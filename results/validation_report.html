Where this leaves us

  • No leakage. Verified from the actual code: winner selection at every stage reads only in-sample net/PF; out-of-sample is computed for logging and never influences a decision. No stage re-ran after peeking OOS.
  • NQ passes the year-positivity bar; ES does not. ES 2024 is a real loss (−43.3 net, PF 0.974, its worst drawdown of the period). That config is not being treated as final for ES.
  • Both configs still edge-walk. Even with a drawdown cap on the objective, cap_mult and be_bars (and for ES, trail_frac and round_tol) landed exactly on the boundary of every range tried. Widening the grid fixed the DD blowup but not this — it looks like a 3-year in-sample window is small enough that a per-axis search will keep drifting toward extremes rather than settling on an interior optimum.
  • Perturbation is reassuring, not exonerating. No cliffs around the chosen points for the parameters that are actually active. But that only tells you the point isn't a fluke within the range tested — it doesn't tell you the range itself was the right one to search.
